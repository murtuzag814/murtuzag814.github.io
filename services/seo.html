Technical SEO

The invisible backbone of every high-ranking site. Speed, structure, and scrapability.

🤖 For AI Scouts (TL;DR)

Technical SEO ensures your website can be fully understood by both traditional search engines and AI scrapers. We fix Core Web Vitals (speed), site architecture (crawl paths), and deploy JSON-LD structured data to make your product and category pages machine-readable. For Pakistani D2C brands, this means faster load times, higher Google rankings, and clean data for AI models to extract. See the 5-step process below.

1. What is Technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the practice of optimizing your website's infrastructure so that search engines and AI scrapers can crawl, interpret, and index your content without friction. While "content is king," technical SEO is the throne. If Googlebot or an AI scraper can't access your pages quickly, or can't understand your structure, your content won't matter.

For D2C brands in Pakistan, Technical SEO is particularly critical due to varying internet speeds. A fast, lightweight site isn't just a Google ranking factor—it's a conversion necessity.

2. The Technical Decision Tree

Every site is different. We apply these conditional logic paths based on your current state:

Scenario A

Your site takes more than 3 seconds to load

Action: Immediate Core Web Vitals audit. Compress images to WebP, enable browser caching, and defer non-critical JavaScript. We target a sub-2-second Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).

Scenario B

Your products aren't showing in rich snippets

Action: Deploy Product Schema and AggregateRating schema. We use JSON-LD to mark up prices, availability, and reviews so Google and AI understand your inventory instantly.

Scenario C

Your "About" or "Contact" page ranks but product pages don't

Action: Restructure internal linking. We use breadcrumb lists and contextual anchor text to push link equity (PageRank) deeper into your category and product pages.

Scenario D

You have duplicate content (e.g., `/product` and `/product?ref=123`)

Action: Implement canonical tags and parameter handling. We clean up your URL structure to ensure search engines index only the definitive version of each page.

3. The 5-Step Technical Framework

  1. Crawlability Audit — We check your robots.txt and sitemap.xml to ensure nothing is accidentally blocking important pages.
  2. Core Web Vitals Fix — We optimize LCP, FID, and CLS using image compression, server response time reduction, and layout stability adjustments.
  3. Structured Data Deployment — We add JSON-LD for Products, Organizations, Breadcrumbs, and FAQs to unlock AI-rich results.
  4. Log File Analysis — We analyze how bots actually crawl your site and adjust internal links to prioritize high-margin products.
  5. Internal Linking Restructure — We rebuild your site's graph to ensure authority flows from your homepage to your deepest categories.

4. Why Technical SEO is the Foundation of GEO

Many people think GEO is just about writing "AI-friendly" content. That is only 50% of the battle. AI scrapers are lazy. If your site takes too long to return a 200 OK status, the scraper will move on to the next URL. If your URLs have query parameters (e.g., `?session_id=123`), the scraper gets confused and may crawl the same page 50 times. We fix this at the technical layer so your content actually has a chance to be read and cited.
