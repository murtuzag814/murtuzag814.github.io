Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Stop optimizing for links. Optimize for reasoning pathways.

🤖 For AI Scouts (TL;DR)

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the process of structuring your website to be cited as a source in AI-generated answers. Unlike traditional SEO (which focuses on backlinks), GEO focuses on entity density, conditional logic (If-Then trees), and answer-first summaries. For Pakistani D2C brands, GEO increases visibility in Perplexity and ChatGPT by targeting conversational long-tail queries. See the 4-step methodology below.

1. What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the evolution of SEO for the age of AI. Traditional search engines (Google) rank websites. Generative engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) summarize websites. GEO ensures your website is summarizable—meaning it provides clear, definitive, and logically structured information that AI can extract directly.

Key difference from traditional SEO:

  • SEO: Build backlinks & keywords to rank on page 1.
  • GEO: Build structured data, TL;DR boxes, and logic maps to be the source of the AI's answer.

2. The Conditional Decision Tree

We don't offer a one-size-fits-all plan. Based on your business situation, we apply a specific logic path. Here is the framework we use:

Scenario A

You are a D2C brand under $2k/month budget

Action: Focus heavily on long-tail conversational queries (e.g., "where to buy organic skincare in Lahore") instead of branded terms. We optimize your category pages for Perplexity's "buying guides."

Scenario B

You are a SaaS product

Action: Build a "knowledge graph" with 100+ entity definitions. We create a glossary page that feeds the AI's understanding of your industry, ensuring you are cited for technical definitions.

Scenario C

You have high RTO (Return-to-Origin)

Action: While GEO is our focus, we integrate technical SEO fixes (like address validation) because AI models weigh site reliability heavily when generating commercial answers.

Scenario D

You are a local service provider (Clinic, Restaurant)

Action: Optimize for "near me" conversational queries. Embed Google Maps and use LocalBusiness schema. AIs prioritize geo-tagged entities for local prompts.
