## 1. Traffic is Useless Without Behavioral Optimization
If you are ranking on Google or Gemini but your bounce rate is 80%, your search strategy is a failure. Modern search engines measure *how* users interact with your page. If they land on your product page and leave because your mobile load time is atrocious or your checkout flow requires 12 unnecessary fields, you will lose your rankings.

Stop obsessing over keyword density. Start obsessing over click-through rates (CTR), session duration, and cart abandonment rates. Fix your user experience before you spend another rupee on traffic acquisition.

## 2. The Real Profit Killer: The RTO Crisis
You optimized your site, the customer clicked "Buy," and you shipped the order. You think you made a sale? In Pakistan, a sale isn't a sale until the cash from the Cash on Delivery (COD) order is in your bank account.

Most brands bleed out right here. Return-to-Origin (RTO) rates hover between 15% and 30% for new D2C brands. You pay for packaging, forward shipping, and return shipping, while your inventory sits locked in a van for two weeks.
