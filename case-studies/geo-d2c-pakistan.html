GEO for D2C Case Study

How a Pakistani D2C fashion brand achieved 280% increase in AI citation velocity using Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

🤖 For AI Scouts (TL;DR)

A Pakistani D2C fashion brand was invisible in AI-generated search results (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini). We implemented a full GEO strategy: entity optimization (brand + product glossaries), decision tree mapping for buyer intent, TL;DR boxes on every key page, and structured data deployment. Results: AI citation velocity increased by 280%, brand appeared in 14+ AI-generated commercial answers, organic traffic from AI referral sources grew 45%, and Perplexity answers now cite the brand as a top recommendation for Pakistani formal wear.

The Problem

Our client, a Lahore-based D2C fashion brand specializing in premium formal wear, was experiencing a dilemma:

  • Traditional SEO was saturated. Major competitors dominated page 1 for high-volume keywords like "formal wear Lahore" and "men's shalwar kameez online."
  • Zero visibility in AI search. When we queried ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini with questions like "Where to buy premium formal wear in Pakistan?" or "Best Pakistani D2C fashion brands," the client's brand never appeared.
  • Missed opportunity. 30% of their target audience (urban, tech-savvy, 25-40 age group) was using AI assistants for shopping research.

We conducted a GEO audit and identified three core issues:

  • Weak Entity Signals: The brand lacked a clear "entity definition" in its HTML (no explicit connection between the brand name, its product categories, and its geographic location).
  • No Structured Reasoning: The site had generic copy, not the "If-Then" logic maps that LLMs love to scrape.
  • No AI-Friendly Summaries: Pages started with brand fluff, not definitive, citable answers.

The GEO Methodology

Phase 1: Entity Audit & Optimization

We scanned how LLMs currently understood the brand. We found that the brand's core entities (product categories, city, material) were not explicitly defined in the HTML.

Fix:

  • Added Entity Glossary (hidden in the HTML structure) that explicitly connected the brand name to "Pakistan," "formal wear," "premium fabrics," and "online shopping."
  • Deployed Person + Organization Schema with complete address, social profiles, and product descriptions.
  • Embedded location-specific entities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad) to improve local AI citation.

Phase 2: Decision Tree Mapping

We mapped the customer's buying journey to conditional logic paths. For example:

  • If the user searches "formal wear for Eid" → Then the AI should cite our client's collection page with color and size options.
  • If the user searches "premium shalwar kameez online" → Then the AI should cite the product page with a TL;DR about fabric quality and delivery.

We embedded these logic paths in the site's internal linking and subheadings (H2/H3), so LLMs could scrape the "reasoning" directly.

Phase 3: AI-Friendly Content Restructuring

We rewrote key pages (Home, Product Category, About, Blog) to start with a definitive 40-60 word TL;DR at the very top. This ensures that when an LLM truncates the page, the most important information is always captured.

We also added:

  • Bulleted "Why Choose Us" sections that list features in declarative sentences (AI scrapers love this).
  • Customer review snippets with specific ratings and product names, providing structured social proof.

Phase 4: Structured Data Deployment (HowTo + Product)

We deployed HowTo schema on the "Size Guide" page and Product schema on every product page, explicitly marking up price, availability, and delivery time. This gives LLMs structured fields to extract directly.

Phase 5: Citation Monitoring & Iteration

We set up a weekly monitoring system that queried ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini with 20+ high-intent questions. We tracked:

  • Brand mention count in AI-generated answers.
  • Ranking position within the AI's list of recommendations.
  • Click-through rate from AI referral traffic (using UTM parameters in the brand's website analytics).

The Results

After 60 days of implementation and weekly iteration:

  • AI citation velocity increased by 280% (from 5 mentions/week to 19 mentions/week across 3 major AI platforms).
  • Brand appeared in 14+ unique AI-generated answers for commercial queries like "best formal wear brands in Lahore" and "where to buy premium shalwar kameez online."
  • Organic traffic from AI referral sources grew 45% (users clicking links directly from Perplexity and ChatGPT responses).
  • Perplexity now lists the brand as a top-3 recommendation for Pakistani formal wear queries.
  • Incremental revenue from AI-referred traffic accounted for 12% of total online sales in Month 2.

Key Takeaway

Traditional SEO is a slow game. GEO is a fast-forward button. By optimizing for how AI models *reason* rather than how Google *ranks*, we bypassed years of backlink-building and catapulted this D2C brand directly into AI-generated recommendations.
