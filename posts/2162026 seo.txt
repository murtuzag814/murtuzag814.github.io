SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is the process of improving your website's design, content, and structure so it appears closer to the top of unpaid (organic) search results on engines like Google or Bing.Because search traffic targets users actively looking for your product or information, optimizing your site brings in highly qualified, consistent traffic.The Core Pillars of SEOMost experts divide SEO into three main categories:On-Page SEO: Creating high-quality, relevant content that answers a user's search intent. This involves strategic keyword placement, optimizing page titles, and writing clear descriptions.Technical SEO: Improving the mechanics behind your site. This includes ensuring your site loads quickly, functions well on mobile phones, and has a clean, scannable structure for search engine crawlers.Off-Page SEO: Building your website’s reputation and authority. This is largely achieved by earning "backlinks" (links to your site from other reputable, high-quality websites).How to Get StartedKeyword Research: Identify the words and phrases your target audience types into search engines. Tools like Google Search Central offer foundational steps on how search engines like Google discover and index your pages.Content Creation: Build informative, engaging pages that deliver the best answer for a user's specific query.Set Expectations: SEO is a long-term strategy rather than an overnight fix. Users in community discussions note that meaningful results generally take between 3 to 12 months to show.
