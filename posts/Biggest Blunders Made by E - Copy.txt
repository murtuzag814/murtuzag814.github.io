### Biggest E-commerce Blunders That Cost Brands Revenue

Many e-commerce businesses focus on growth while ignoring the mistakes that quietly reduce profits. The most common issues I see are:

1. **Poor audience understanding** — weak targeting and messaging lead to low conversion rates.
2. **Poor product pages** — unclear descriptions, weak visuals, and lack of trust signals hurt sales.
3. **Ignoring mobile users** — slow, non-optimized mobile experiences drive customers away.
4. **No conversion tracking** — without data, it's impossible to optimize marketing or measure ROI.
5. **Complicated checkout** — extra steps and long forms increase cart abandonment.
6. **Weak customer retention** — relying only on new customers limits long-term growth.
7. **Poor inventory management** — stockouts and overstocking both damage profitability.

Fixing these fundamentals often delivers bigger gains than increasing ad spend.
