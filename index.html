Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about e-commerce SEO, GEO, AEO, and working with Ghulam Murtuza.

What does an e-commerce SEO consultant in Karachi do?

An e-commerce SEO consultant in Karachi like Ghulam Murtuza helps Pakistani online stores and D2C brands rank higher on Google, appear in AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity, and convert that traffic into sales. Services include technical SEO, on-page optimization, keyword strategy, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization).

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and why do Pakistani brands need it?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the process of optimizing your brand's digital presence so AI search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude — cite and recommend your brand in their answers. When a customer asks "best baby clothes brand in Karachi" in ChatGPT, GEO determines whether your brand appears. Almost no Pakistani brand is investing in GEO yet — making it the highest-ROI SEO opportunity available right now.

How did Ghulam Murtuza reduce RTO by 40% for a Pakistani fashion brand?

After analysing 50,000+ orders at a leading Pakistani fashion D2C brand, Ghulam Murtuza identified three root causes of high RTO: fake or incomplete addresses, low customer intent on COD orders, and late delivery attempts. He implemented address verification at checkout (−22% RTO), pre-delivery confirmation calls (−15% RTO), and COD order capping based on customer history (−18% RTO). Combined with courier reconciliation, these changes saved millions in reverse logistics costs.

Does Ghulam Murtuza work with brands outside Karachi?

Yes. While GMurtuza SEO is based in Karachi, all services are available remotely to brands across Pakistan — Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, and beyond. Ghulam Murtuza currently works with clients in multiple cities and can also travel for in-person workshops and strategy sessions.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) gets your brand ranking on Google's traditional blue-link results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) gets your brand featured in Google's answer boxes, featured snippets, and voice search responses. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude. A complete Search Everywhere Optimization strategy — as practiced by Ghulam Murtuza — covers all three.
