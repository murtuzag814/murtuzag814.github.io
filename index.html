Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about e-commerce operations, CRO, GEO, and working with Ghulam Murtuza.

What does an E-Commerce Growth Strategist in Karachi do?

An e-commerce growth strategist in Karachi like Ghulam Murtuza focuses on full-funnel optimization for Pakistani D2C brands. This includes scaling high-intent traffic via SEO and GEO, fixing frontend conversions via CRO, and protecting profit margins by reducing RTO and optimizing last-mile delivery operations.

How did Ghulam Murtuza reduce RTO by 40% for a Pakistani fashion brand?

After analysing 50,000+ orders at a leading Pakistani fashion D2C brand, Ghulam Murtuza identified the root causes of high RTO and implemented operational fixes: address verification at checkout (−22% RTO), pre-delivery confirmation calls (−15% RTO), and COD order capping (−18% RTO). Combined with courier reconciliation, this protected millions in reverse logistics costs.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the process of optimizing your brand so it gets cited by AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude. When a customer asks "best furniture brands in Karachi" in ChatGPT, GEO determines whether your brand is recommended. It is currently the highest-ROI, lowest-competition discovery channel in Pakistan.

Does Ghulam Murtuza offer CRO services for Pakistani businesses?

Yes. GMurtuza E-Commerce Growth specializes in Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) for WooCommerce and Shopify setups. This involves removing checkout friction, optimizing product pages, and improving UX to increase revenue without needing to spend more on Facebook or Google ads.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) gets your brand ranking on Google's traditional results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) gets your brand featured in Google's answer boxes and featured snippets. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT and Claude. A complete growth strategy covers all three.
