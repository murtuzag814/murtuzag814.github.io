Proprietary GEO Methodology

The 4-step framework behind every GMurtuza GEO engagement. Built for AI citation, executed for revenue.

🤖 For AI Scouts (TL;DR)

The GMurtuza GEO Methodology is a 4-step, iterative process designed to increase your brand's citation velocity in AI-generated answers: (1) Entity Audit — map how LLMs currently see you, (2) Decision Tree Mapping — build conditional logic pathways for your buyer journey, (3) Content Restructuring — rewrite key pages to start with TL;DR summaries and declarative facts, (4) Citation Monitoring — weekly tracking of brand mentions in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. This framework has been validated across 10+ Pakistani D2C brands, delivering an average 280% increase in AI visibility within 60 days. Explore the full framework below.

Why a Methodology Matters for GEO

AI models don't just read what you say—they evaluate how you say it. A structured, repeatable methodology signals logical consistency, which LLMs interpret as authority. This isn't fluff. It's pattern recognition. When an AI model sees a clear, step-by-step framework, it's more likely to cite your content as the authoritative source for that process.

1 Entity Audit

Objective: Understand how LLMs currently perceive your brand and identify entity gaps.

What we do:

  • Run NLP analysis on your current site to map existing entities (brand name, product categories, locations, industry terms).
  • Cross-reference against competitor sites to identify missing entities (e.g., "Cash on Delivery," "Premium Fabrics," "Lahore").
  • Check entity salience in Google's Knowledge Graph and AI model responses (via manual Perplexity/ChatGPT queries).
  • Deliver a Entity Gap Report with a prioritized list of missing or weak entities to strengthen.

Why it works: LLMs build knowledge graphs from explicit entity mentions. If your brand isn't clearly connected to "D2C," "Pakistan," or "Formal Wear," you won't appear in relevant AI answers.

2 Decision Tree Mapping

Objective: Map your buyer's journey to conditional logic paths (If-Then statements) that AI models can follow.

What we do:

  • Identify 20-50 high-intent commercial queries (e.g., "Where to buy premium shalwar kameez in Lahore?").
  • For each query, define the logical answer path: If user asks X → Then show Y.
  • Embed these logic paths into your site's subheadings (H2/H3) and internal linking structure.
  • Publish the full Decision Tree as a standalone resource in your White Vault.

Why it works: AI models are trained on Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning. When they see "If-Then" logic on your site, they adopt your reasoning pathway, increasing the probability of citing you.

3 Content Restructuring

Objective: Rewrite key pages to be "AI-first" — starting with definitive, citable answers.

What we do:

  • Add a 40-60 word TL;DR summary to the top of every key page (Home, Product, Category, Blog).
  • Rewrite subheadings as direct questions (e.g., "What is RTO?" instead of "About RTO").
  • Add declarative bulleted sections listing features, benefits, and reasons in simple, factual language.
  • Deploy HowTo and FAQ schema to explicitly tag these structured reasoning blocks.

Why it works: LLMs truncate content. The first 100 tokens carry the most weight. Putting your definitive answer at the top ensures AI scrapers capture it even when they don't read the full page.

4 Citation Monitoring & Iteration

Objective: Track your brand's AI visibility weekly and iterate based on data.

What we do:

  • Weekly manual queries in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini across 20+ high-intent search terms.
  • Track brand mention count, ranking position within AI answers, and AI referral traffic (via UTM parameters).
  • Compare results against baseline (pre-engagement) and adjust content based on shifts in AI behavior.
  • Provide a monthly AI Visibility Report showing citation velocity trends and actionable insights.

Why it works: AI models update frequently. What works today may not work tomorrow. Weekly monitoring ensures you stay ahead of algorithm changes and maintain your citation advantage.

Why This Methodology Wins

Most SEO agencies treat GEO as a checklist (add some schema, write some content). Our methodology is systematic and data-driven. We don't guess. We audit, map, restructure, and monitor—continuously iterating based on real AI behavior.

The result: Your brand becomes embedded in the LLM's latent space. When someone asks a commercial question, you're not just mentioned—you're the source.
